OUR RESEARCH

Built around what local organisations actually need.

We spoke with local community organisations to understand how volunteers discover opportunities, what makes participation difficult, and what NGOs need from a volunteer platform.

Organisations we consulted

Vrikshit Nagpur

Area of work: Community environment and local action

Placeholder summary: We discussed how volunteers discover local opportunities, what keeps people from joining, and how organisations need clearer communication before events.

Love And Care

Area of work: Health and Social Care

Placeholder summary: Support community health and social care initiatives by assisting with outreach, awareness activities, and community support programs.

Nagpur United

Area of work: Charity and Donation Drives

Placeholder summary: Support charitable initiatives by helping organise donation drives, collect essential supplies, and distribute resources to communities in need.

What we learned

Discoverability

Volunteers often struggle to find suitable local opportunities.

Coordination

NGOs need an easier way to communicate opportunities and manage participation.

Volunteer fit

Location, schedule and type of activity matter when volunteers choose an opportunity.

Trust & clarity

Volunteers need clear information about the organisation, activity and expectations before participating.

These conversations helped shape the features and direction of Bridgefinity.

NEW TO VOLUNTEERING?

Connect Contribute And Create Impact.

You do not need experience to make a useful contribution. Here is what to expect before you take your first step.

Browse beginner-friendly opportunities →
What do I actually do?▼
Each listing explains the activity, your role and what to bring. An NGO coordinator will guide you when you arrive, so you can focus on helping rather than figuring everything out alone.
Is this trustworthy?▼
We show the NGO behind every opportunity and the details they provide. Look for clear activity information, a named location and a coordinator contact before you apply.
Is this safe?▼
Choose activities with complete details and meet at the listed public location. Follow the organiser's instructions, tell someone where you are going and contact the coordinator if plans change.
Will someone respond?▼
Yes. After you apply, the NGO coordinator reviews your request and confirms the next steps. You will know where to go, when to arrive and who to look for.
